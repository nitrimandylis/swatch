# Batman Jazz palette

Pulled from `batman jazz.jpg`. Mostly a near-black room with steel-blue
midtones, a crimson city sky out the window, and the neon-pink bat-signal as
the one loud accent.

## Roles

| Role      | Hex       | From                      |
|-----------|-----------|---------------------------|
| base (bg) | `#09090b` | the dark room             |
| surface   | `#122031` | raised panels / walls     |
| overlay   | `#294a6d` | deep steel                |
| text (fg) | `#c5d3e0` | cool off-white            |
| muted     | `#5f7897` | steel-blue shadow         |
| accent    | `#e85a9c` | neon-pink bat-signal      |
| crimson   | `#8f2a3a` | city sky                  |

## ANSI 16

|         | normal    | bright    |
|---------|-----------|-----------|
| black   | `#20252f` | `#5f7897` |
| red     | `#a23a4a` | `#c85566` |
| green   | `#7d9b6f` | `#94b586` |
| yellow  | `#c9a76a` | `#dcc088` |
| blue    | `#4e749e` | `#678dae` |
| magenta | `#b06a8f` | `#e85a9c` |
| cyan    | `#4a8f9e` | `#67aab8` |
| white   | `#c5d3e0` | `#e8eef5` |

Green and cyan aren't really in the image, so I made them up and kept them cool
and desaturated to blend in. Yellow is the nightstand lamp. Bright magenta is
the bat-signal.
