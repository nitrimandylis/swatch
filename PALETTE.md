# Batman Jazz — palette

Moody neon-noir, derived from `batman jazz.jpg`. Near-black room, steel-blue
midtones, crimson city sky, one hot neon-pink bat-signal as the accent.

## Roles

| Role      | Hex       | From                        |
|-----------|-----------|-----------------------------|
| base (bg) | `#09090b` | dark room (dominant, 69%)   |
| surface   | `#122031` | raised panels / walls       |
| overlay   | `#294a6d` | deep steel                  |
| text (fg) | `#c5d3e0` | cool off-white              |
| muted     | `#5f7897` | steel-blue shadow           |
| accent    | `#e85a9c` | **neon pink bat-signal**    |
| crimson   | `#8f2a3a` | city sky                    |
